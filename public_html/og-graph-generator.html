                Frequently Asked Questions
            

            
                    

                        

                            What is the Open Graph protocol?
                        

                        
Open Graph is a set of meta tags originally created by Facebook that lets you control how your content appears when shared on social media platforms.

                    

                    

                        

                            What image size works best for Open Graph?
                        

                        
The recommended Open Graph image size is 1200×630 pixels at a 1.91:1 ratio. This ensures your image displays correctly on all platforms including Facebook and LinkedIn.

                    

                    

                        

                            Do Open Graph tags affect Google SEO?
                        

                        
OG tags don't directly affect Google rankings, but they increase social sharing and engagement, which can drive traffic and earn backlinks — indirectly boosting SEO.

                    

                    

                        

                            Which platforms use Open Graph tags?
                        

                        
Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Slack, Discord, WhatsApp (for link previews), and most other social platforms read Open Graph meta tags.
