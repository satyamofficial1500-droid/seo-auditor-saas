How to Submit sitemap.xml to Google Search Console

An XML sitemap acts as a roadmap for search engines like Google, Bing, and Yandex. Submitting your sitemap ensures search engine crawlers index all your important landing pages quickly.

Step 1: Download your generated sitemap.xml

Click the download button above to save your formatted sitemap.xml file to your computer.

Step 2: Upload sitemap.xml to Your Website Root Folder

Upload the file to your web server root directory via FTP or File Manager so it is accessible at https://yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml.

Step 3: Submit in Google Search Console

Log in to Google Search Console, select your property, click Sitemaps in the left menu, enter sitemap.xml in the input field, and click Submit.
