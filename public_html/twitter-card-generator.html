                Frequently Asked Questions
            

            
                    

                        

                            What is a Twitter Card?
                        

                        
A Twitter Card is a rich preview that appears when someone shares your URL on X (Twitter). It shows an image, title, and description instead of just a plain link.

                    

                    

                        

                            What's the difference between Summary and Summary Large Image?
                        

                        
Summary shows a small square thumbnail. Summary Large Image shows a large banner-style image (2:1 ratio, minimum 300×157px recommended 1200×628px).

                    

                    

                        

                            Do Twitter Card tags overlap with Open Graph tags?
                        

                        
Yes, both serve similar purposes but for different platforms. Many tags can share values — it's best to include both sets for maximum social media coverage.

                    

                    

                        

                            How do I validate my Twitter Card?
                        

                        
Use the X (Twitter) Card Validator at cards-dev.twitter.com/validator to preview and validate your card tags before publishing.
