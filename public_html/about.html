About SEO Error Auditor

Empowering webmasters, agencies, and store owners with 100% free, enterprise-grade technical SEO tools.

Who We Are

SEO Error Auditor (seoerrorauditor.com) is an automated technical SEO platform created to help web developers, digital marketers, and business owners diagnose and fix critical website health errors before they hurt Google search rankings.

What Makes Us Different

  • No Page Limits: Unlike traditional tools that limit free scans to 500 pages, our crawler lets you generate sitemaps and audit up to 10,000+ pages for free.
  • Instant 11-Sheet Excel Reports: Download detailed multi-tab reports detailing 404 broken links, missing title tags, duplicate canonicals, and thin content pages.
  • Developer IDE Compilers: Built-in real-time IDE code compilers for JSON-LD Schemas, Open Graph cards, Meta Tags, Robots.txt, and AI LLMs.txt files.

Multi-Threaded Engine Architecture

Our core crawling engine is powered by Python multi-threaded workers capable of processing up to 20 concurrent HTTP requests per second with automatic exponential backoff, SSL validation, and robots.txt compliance.
