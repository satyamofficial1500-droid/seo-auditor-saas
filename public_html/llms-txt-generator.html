                Frequently Asked Questions
            

            
                    

                        

                            What is llms.txt?
                        

                        
llms.txt is an emerging standard (inspired by robots.txt) that tells AI language models like ChatGPT and Claude how they can interact with your website content — including crawling, training, and citation rules.

                    

                    

                        

                            Which AI models read llms.txt?
                        

                        
Support is growing. Currently, AI crawlers from Anthropic (Claude), some GPT-based crawlers, and Perplexity AI have indicated support for llms.txt directives.

                    

                    

                        

                            Is llms.txt an official standard?
                        

                        
llms.txt is a community-proposed standard (not yet an official RFC), but it's rapidly gaining adoption among website owners who want to control AI content usage.

                    

                    

                        

                            Does llms.txt affect Google rankings?
                        

                        
No, llms.txt has no effect on traditional search engine crawling or rankings. It specifically targets AI language model crawlers and training pipelines.
